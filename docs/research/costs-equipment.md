## 9. "Is a pool service worth it" (DIY vs hiring)

- Pro service about $960 to $1,800 a year (HomeGuide, Angi; rows 1 and 6).
- DIY: Forbes Advisor gives $5 to $15 a month for DIY on an above-ground pool vs $80 to $150 a month for a pro (row 10). Other DIY chemical estimates in the summary ranged from about $100 a year to $30 to $75 a month, with $100 to $300 for starter tools; attribution among Angi, Bob Vila and HomeGuide uncertain. Sources: Bob Vila, "How Much Does It Cost to Maintain a Pool?", https://www.bobvila.com/articles/pool-maintenance-cost/ ; Angi maintenance page above (verified via search summary, attribution uncertain).
- Time: the only hours figure found was an Angi city-level page: 60 to 90 minutes per weekly visit (about 50 to 75 hours a year). City page, not national: use only as "about an hour or more a week" with attribution to Angi, or leave off.
- Several guides note DIY can save up to about $1,000 a year but caution about handling chemicals (attribution uncertain).
- No independent, non-commercial comparison study was found.

## 10. Maintenance schedule basics

- **Pool & Hot Tub Alliance (industry association) consumer page:** weekly, test chlorine, pH, alkalinity and other levels with a test kit and adjust; brush walls and floor weekly to prevent algae and stains; vacuum dirt and debris. Monthly: backwash sand filters or clean cartridges as needed; shock (oxidize); check pumps, filters and heaters for wear. Quarterly: test total dissolved solids and clean tile and grout. Keep a log of test results and maintenance. PHTA, "Maintaining Your Pool", https://www.phta.org/consumer/maintenance/maintaining-your-pool/ (verified via search summary)
- **CDC:** keep pH 7.0 to 7.8 and free chlorine at least 1 ppm (2 ppm with stabilizer); see licensing-safety.md item 17. CDC testing frequency for home pools not confirmed (see that file).
- **Filter pressure:** retailer guides say backwash sand/DE filters when pressure rises about 8 to 10 psi above the clean reading; cartridge filters are removed and rinsed instead. Retailer blogs only: do not cite; prefer "follow the filter manufacturer's directions".
- No university extension pool schedule surfaced.
