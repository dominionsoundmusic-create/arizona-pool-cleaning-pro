- Date: 2026-10-21
- Method: verified via search summary. WebFetch was tried once (census.gov) and failed (host not resolvable), so every fact below was checked against the WebSearch result summary of the named page. The URL recorded is the page whose content the search showed.

## Yuma (city)

- **Status:** incorporated city; county seat of Yuma County. The city charter under the laws of the new state of Arizona took effect April 7, 1914. The city site says the town was first incorporated under territorial law as Arizona City in 1871 and renamed Yuma in 1873. The state commerce profile gives founded 1854, incorporated 1914.
  - Source: City of Yuma, About Yuma, https://www.yumaaz.gov/visitors/about-yuma (verified via search summary)
  - Source: Arizona Commerce Authority, Community Profile for Yuma, https://www.azcommerce.com/a/profiles/ViewProfile/144/Yuma/ (verified via search summary)
  - Safe wording: "Yuma's city charter took effect in 1914." Avoid stating a single founding year, since sources differ (1854 vs 1871).
- **Elevation:** 138 feet.
  - Source: Arizona Commerce Authority, Community Profile for Yuma, https://www.azcommerce.com/a/profiles/ViewProfile/144/Yuma/ (verified via search summary)
- **Population:** 95,548 (2020 Census, April 1, 2020); 93,064 (2010 Census). Estimates: 103,559 (July 1, 2024, V2024); 105,227 (July 1, 2025, V2025).
- **Land area:** 120.67 square miles; 791.8 people per square mile (2020).
  - Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts: Yuma city, Arizona, https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/yumacityarizona/PST045225 (verified via search summary)
- **Language:** 47.2% of persons age 5+ speak a language other than English at home (ACS 2020-2024). Earlier window: 46.9% (2018-2022).
  - Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts: Yuma city, Arizona, https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/yumacityarizona/NES010223 and https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/yumacityarizona,US/PST045222 (verified via search summary)
- **Landmarks (official sources):**
  - Yuma Territorial Prison State Historic Park sits on a bluff overlooking the Colorado River. The first seven inmates arrived July 1, 1876; the prison closed in 1909 because of overcrowding.
    - Source: Arizona State Parks and Trails, Yuma Territorial Prison State Historic Park, https://azstateparks.com/yuma-territorial (verified via search summary); Yuma Crossing National Heritage Area, Yuma Territorial Prison, https://www.yumaheritage.com/yuma-territorial-prison.html (verified via search summary)
  - Colorado River State Historic Park sits on part of the grounds of the old U.S. Army Quartermaster Depot, established 1864; renamed Colorado River State Historic Park in October 2017.
    - Source: Arizona State Parks and Trails, Colorado River State Historic Park, Park History, https://azstateparks.com/colorado-river/about-the-park/park-history (verified via search summary)
  - Yuma Crossing National Heritage Area manages both parks for the City of Yuma under a 15-year agreement with Arizona State Parks and Trails signed in 2016.
    - Source: National Park Service, Yuma Crossing National Heritage Area, https://www.nps.gov/places/yuma-crossing-national-heritage-area.htm (verified via search summary)
  - Military: Marine Corps Air Station Yuma and U.S. Army Yuma Proving Ground are both in the Yuma area; a Yuma County page calls Yuma Proving Ground the county's largest single civilian employer. Visit Yuma describes MCAS Yuma as the Marine Corps' busiest air station.
    - Source: Yuma County, Economic Development Basics, https://www.yumacountyaz.gov/government/county-administrator/economic-development-plans (verified via search summary); Visit Yuma, Military, https://www.visityuma.com/about-yuma/military/ (verified via search summary)
    - Caution: headcount and dollar-impact figures for the bases vary by source and are dated; do not use them.

## Fortuna Foothills ("the Foothills")

- **Status:** unincorporated community and census-designated place (CDP). Listed among the unincorporated communities of Yuma County (with Gadsden) in a Yuma County Intergovernmental Public Transportation Authority notice.
  - Source: Visit Yuma, Yuma County Intergovernmental Public Transportation Authority listing, https://www.visityuma.com/listing/yuma-county-intergovernmental-public-transportation-authority/63/ (verified via search summary)
  - Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts: Fortuna Foothills CDP, Arizona, https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fortunafoothillscdparizona/HCN010217 (verified via search summary)
- **Location:** east of Yuma. Interstate 8 runs through the community: a Yuma County news item describes a South Foothills loop (south of the interstate) and a North Foothills section (north of the interstate). Foothills Community Park is at 13340 S. Frontage Road.
  - Source: Visit Yuma, Location: Foothills, https://www.visityuma.com/location/foothills (verified via search summary)
  - Source: Yuma County, Bike Routes to debut in Foothills, https://www.yumacountyaz.gov/Home/Components/News/News/1095/ (verified via search summary)
  - Source: Yuma County, Parks, https://www.yumacountyaz.gov/government/parks-and-recreation (verified via search summary)
- **Winter visitors:** Visit Yuma: "The Foothills," formerly known as Fortuna Foothills, is "a community largely comprised of winter visitors, to the east of Yuma," and it "has become increasingly popular for permanent residents of the area as well." Visit Yuma lists Fortuna de Oro RV Resort there as an active 55+ community with 1,200 sites (do not name the resort on the site; the "large 55+ RV resorts" point is usable).
  - Source: Visit Yuma, Location: Foothills, https://www.visityuma.com/location/foothills (verified via search summary); Visit Yuma, Fortuna de Oro RV Resort listing, https://www.visityuma.com/listing/fortuna-de-oro-rv-resort/65/ (verified via search summary)
- **Population:** 27,776 (2020 Census); 26,265 (2010 Census). About 40 square miles of land; about 693 people per square mile (2020). No current-year estimate shown for the CDP.
- **Housing (ACS 2020-2024):** owner-occupied housing rate 83.6%; median value of owner-occupied homes $199,200; 13,615 households; about 2.12 persons per household.
  - Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts: Fortuna Foothills CDP, Arizona, https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/fortunafoothillscdparizona/HCN010217 (verified via search summary)

## Somerton (city)

- **Status:** incorporated city. Established 1898, incorporated 1918.
- **Location:** in the Yuma Valley in southwestern Yuma County, about 12 miles south of Yuma. Elevation 103 feet.
- **Economy:** mainly agricultural, with medical services and tourism also important. Known for its annual Tamale Festival in December.
  - Source: Arizona Commerce Authority, Community Profile for Somerton, https://www.azcommerce.com/a/profiles/ViewProfile/115/Somerton/ (verified via search summary); City of Somerton, Welcome to Somerton, AZ, https://www.somertonaz.gov/i_want_to/learn_about/quality_of_life.php (verified via search summary)
- **Population:** 14,197 (2020 Census); 14,287 (2010 Census).
- **Language:** 90.5% of persons age 5+ speak a language other than English at home (ACS 2020-2024); 88.7% (2019-2023).
  - Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts: Somerton city, Arizona, https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/somertoncityarizona (verified via search summary) (the 2020-2024 figure was shown on a QuickFacts multi-place comparison table; cite this single-city URL)

## San Luis (city)

- **Status:** incorporated city. Founded 1930, incorporated 1979. About 30 square miles. Elevation 100 feet.
  - Source: City of San Luis, About, https://sanluisaz.gov/government/about/index.php (verified via search summary); Arizona Commerce Authority, Community Profile for San Luis, https://www.azcommerce.com/a/profiles/ViewProfile/107/San+Luis/ (verified via search summary)
- **Location:** in the southwestern corner of Arizona, at the international border, reached from Yuma by following U.S. Highway 95 south. The San Luis I Port of Entry is one of Arizona's busiest ports.
  - Source: Visit Yuma, Helpful Links / Going south from Yuma, https://www.visityuma.com/about-yuma/resources/ (verified via search summary); Arizona Commerce Authority, Community Profile for San Luis (above)
  - Distance: Visit Yuma states "about 30 miles from downtown Yuma." Caution: this tourism figure looks high; prefer "south of Yuma on U.S. 95" with no number.
- **Population:** 35,257 (2020 Census); 25,505 (2010 Census); 42,030 (July 1, 2025 estimate).
- **Language:** 90.9% of persons age 5+ speak a language other than English at home (ACS 2020-2024); 91.9% (2019-2023).
  - Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts: San Luis city, Arizona, https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/sanluiscityarizona/PST045224 (verified via search summary)

## Wellton (town)

- **Status:** incorporated town. Founded 1904, incorporated 1970. Name comes from "Well Town," after wells drilled to supply water to the Southern Pacific Railroad.
- **Location:** about 29 miles east of Yuma along Interstate 8.
- **Setting:** economy traditionally based on agriculture and ranching; population includes many winter and retirement residents. Serves as a business, service and recreation center for more than 5,000 people in the Wellton-Mohawk Valley and surrounding area.
- **Population (as given by the state profile):** 1,829 (2000); 2,884 (2010); about 2,482 (2023 estimate).
  - Source: Arizona Commerce Authority, Community Profile for Wellton, https://www.azcommerce.com/a/profiles/ViewProfile/135/Wellton/ (verified via search summary)

## Yuma County (overall)

- **County seat:** Yuma.
  - Source: Yuma County, About the County, https://www.yumacountyaz.gov/visitors/about-the-county (verified via search summary)
- **Founded:** November 9, 1864, one of Arizona's four original counties created by the 1st Arizona Territorial Legislature. La Paz County was split off by voters in 1983.
  - Source: Yuma County, About the County, https://www.yumacountyaz.gov/visitors/about-the-county (verified via search summary)
- **Population:** 203,881 (2020 Census, April 1, 2020). Estimates: 220,310 (July 1, 2024); 224,449 (July 1, 2025).
- **Land area:** 5,513.84 square miles (2020). (County documents also cite 5,522 square miles total area; use the Census land figure.)
- **Language:** 55.5% of persons age 5+ speak a language other than English at home (ACS 2020-2024); 54.5% (2019-2023).
  - Source: U.S. Census Bureau, QuickFacts: Yuma County, Arizona, https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/yumacountyarizona/PST045224 and https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/yumacityarizona,yumacountyarizona/PST045222 (verified via search summary)
  - Note: the county table showed an "estimates base" of 205,006 for April 2020; that is not the Census count. Use 203,881 as the 2020 Census figure.
- **Agriculture:** University of Arizona Cooperative Extension: "Yuma is known as the Winter Salad Bowl of the nation, producing over 90% of the winter leafy greens and vegetables." Yuma County's economic development page also cites 90% of the U.S. supply of winter vegetables. A November 2024 extension report says Yuma County is part of a seasonal rotation of leafy greens production that supplies most of the nation's leafy greens, including iceberg, romaine, green leaf, red leaf and Boston lettuce, with Western Arizona producing in the winter months.
  - Source: University of Arizona Cooperative Extension, AZ1615 Planting and Harvesting Calendar for Gardeners in Yuma, https://extension.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2024-08/az1615-2020.pdf (verified via search summary)
  - Source: Yuma County, Economic Development Basics, https://www.yumacountyaz.gov/government/county-administrator/economic-development-plans (verified via search summary)
  - Source: University of Arizona Cooperative Extension, Economic Contribution of Yuma County Agriculture (November 2024), https://extension.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2025-02/Economic-Contribution-of-Yuma-County-Agriculture-Report.pdf (verified via search summary)
  - Safe wording: "Yuma is known as the nation's Winter Salad Bowl" (attributed to UA Extension). If using "over 90%," attribute it to UA Extension.
