About Yuma Pool Cleaning Pro

Local Service in the Hottest City in Arizona

Yuma Pool Cleaning Pro is a residential pool service company covering Yuma, the Foothills, Fortuna Foothills, Somerton, and San Luis. We handle weekly cleaning, water chemistry, equipment repair, and maintenance for pools running in the most extreme heat in Arizona — 175 days above 100 degrees and summer highs that regularly push past 115.

We are not a franchise and we are not a scheduling app. The same technician services your pool every visit, knows your equipment, and notices when something changes before it becomes a repair bill.

What We Do

Weekly pool cleaning with chemicals included — skimming, brushing, vacuuming, chemistry testing and balancing, filter and equipment checks. One flat monthly price. In Yuma, where chemical consumption is higher than anywhere else in Arizona because of the extreme heat, included chemicals protect you from unpredictable monthly costs.

Equipment repair — pumps, motors, filters, heaters, salt cells, lights, and plumbing leaks. Diagnosed on site, priced before work starts.

Snowbird service — year-round maintenance that keeps the pool ready while you are away. No green-water cleanup when you return.

Why Yuma Is Different

Yuma pools consume more chlorine, handle more dust and debris, and put more stress on equipment than pools in any other Arizona city. The water from the Colorado River is hard. The agricultural dust loads pools daily. The heat pushes water temperatures past 95 degrees, which more than doubles chlorine demand. Our service is built around these realities — our chemical dosing, our inspection checklist, and our visit focus all account for what Yuma's climate does to a pool.

Service Area

We run routes through central Yuma, the Foothills, Fortuna Foothills, Somerton, and San Luis. If your pool is in Yuma County, we can reach you. Call with your address and pool size for a flat monthly price.
