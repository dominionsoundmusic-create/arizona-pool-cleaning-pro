About Yuma Pool Cleaning Pro

Local Service in the Hottest City in Arizona

Yuma Pool Cleaning Pro is a residential pool service company covering Yuma, the Foothills, Fortuna Foothills, Somerton, and San Luis. A good pool service handles weekly cleaning, water chemistry, equipment repair, and maintenance for pools running in the most extreme heat in Arizona — 175 days above 100 degrees and summer highs that regularly push past 115.

We are not a franchise and we are not a scheduling app. The same technician services your pool every visit, knows your equipment, and notices when something changes before it becomes a repair bill.

What Gets Done

Weekly pool cleaning — skimming, brushing, vacuuming, chemistry testing and balancing, filter and equipment checks. Ask what the monthly price includes. In Yuma, where chemical consumption is higher than anywhere else in Arizona because of the extreme heat, included chemicals protect you from unpredictable monthly costs.

Equipment repair — pumps, motors, filters, heaters, salt cells, lights, and plumbing leaks. Diagnosed on site, priced before work starts.

Snowbird service — year-round maintenance that keeps the pool ready while you are away. No green-water cleanup when you return.

Why Yuma Is Different

Yuma pools consume more chlorine, handle more dust and debris, and put more stress on equipment than pools in any other Arizona city. The water from the Colorado River is hard. The agricultural dust loads pools daily. The heat pushes water temperatures past 95 degrees, which more than doubles chlorine demand. A good pool service in Yuma adjusts its chemical dosing, inspection checklist and visit focus for what this climate does to a pool.

Service Area

Calls are taken from central Yuma, the Foothills, Fortuna Foothills, Somerton, and San Luis. Calls are taken from anywhere in Yuma County. Call with your address and pool size for a monthly price.
