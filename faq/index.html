Monthly pricing depends on pool size, spa, and sanitization type. Call with your address and pool size for a flat monthly quote. Chemicals included, no per-visit charges.

Yuma Climate & Water

Why does a Yuma pool need more chemicals than other cities?

Yuma averages 175 days above 100 degrees. Water temperatures above 95 degrees more than double chlorine consumption. Extreme evaporation concentrates minerals faster. Agricultural dust feeds algae. All three mean higher chemical usage than Phoenix or Tucson.

Do you service snowbird pools?

Yes. We offer year-round service that keeps the pool maintained while you are away. When you return, the pool is ready — no green water cleanup, no surprise repair bill from equipment sitting idle in 120-degree heat.
