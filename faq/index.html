Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common pool cleaning and maintenance questions for Yuma homeowners.

Service & Pricing

What is included in weekly pool service?

Every visit includes skimming, brushing, vacuuming, emptying baskets, testing and adjusting water chemistry, checking filter pressure, and inspecting equipment. Ask whether routine chemicals are included in the monthly price.

Are chemicals included in the price?

It depends on the company. Many pool services include routine chemicals such as chlorine, acid and stabilizer in the monthly price, while others bill them separately. Ask on the call and get the answer in writing.

How much does pool service cost in Yuma?

Monthly pricing depends on pool size, spa, and sanitization type. Call with your address and pool size for a flat monthly quote. Ask what the monthly price includes.

Yuma Climate & Water

Why does a Yuma pool need more chemicals than other cities?

Yuma averages 175 days above 100 degrees. Water temperatures above 95 degrees more than double chlorine consumption. Extreme evaporation concentrates minerals faster. Agricultural dust feeds algae. All three mean higher chemical usage than Phoenix or Tucson.

Do you service snowbird pools?

Yes. A good pool service offers year-round service that keeps the pool maintained while you are away. When you return, the pool is ready — no green water cleanup, no surprise repair bill from equipment sitting idle in 120-degree heat.
