What to say when you call

A few plain sentences are enough. The pool company hears a short summary of what you say before it picks up.

Where you are

Yuma, Fortuna Foothills, Somerton, San Luis, Wellton or Gadsden. A cross street or neighborhood helps too.

What the pool is doing

For example: green water, a loud pump, a leak at the filter, crust on the tile, or a pool that sat all summer.

What kind of pool it is

Plaster or pebble, chlorine or saltwater, with or without a heater, if you know. If not, the company will see it.

What happens next

After a few questions, the assistant puts you through to a Yuma-area pool business with local owners, which is separate from the line. If that business is busy, the assistant forwards your name, callback number, community and pool problem for a return call. Keep your phone close and pick up unknown numbers for a while.

From then on you deal with the company directly. It inspects the pool, sets its price, does the job once you say yes, and bills you. Before any repair, write down its ROC license number and look that number up in the ROC contractor search (new tab). Nobody at the line verifies licenses. The how it works page lists more questions to ask.

Call 911 first in an emergency

Gas smell near a pool heater: leave the area right away. Do not use switches, flames or anything that could make a spark. From a safe distance, call 911 and Southwest Gas at 877-860-6020.

A child or anyone in trouble in the water: call 911 now. Then keep children away from the pool until a fence, gate or cover is secure.

Pool chemicals should never be combined. Store each one in the container it came in, out of reach of children and pets. If you are unsure about a chemical or electrical problem, leave it alone and describe it to the pool company.

Your details and privacy

What you tell the assistant, or type into the form, goes to the pool company that helps you, so it can call you back. Calls may be recorded. The privacy policy explains what is kept, who receives it and why.

Areas the line covers

The line takes calls from the City of Yuma and nearby Yuma County communities. The service areas page has notes on each one.
