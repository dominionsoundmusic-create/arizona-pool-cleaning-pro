The only way to reach Yuma Pool Cleaning Pro is by phone. Tap the button on a mobile phone, or dial the number from any phone.

What to say when you call

A few plain sentences are enough. The pool company hears a short summary of what you say before it picks up.

Where you are

Yuma, Fortuna Foothills, Somerton, San Luis, Wellton or Gadsden. A cross street or neighborhood helps too.

What the pool is doing

For example: green water, a loud pump, a leak at the filter, crust on the tile, or a pool that sat all summer.

What kind of pool it is

Plaster or pebble, chlorine or saltwater, with or without a heater, if you know. If not, the company will see it.

What happens next

After a few questions, the assistant transfers you to an independent, locally owned pool service company in the Yuma area. If that company is busy, your name, callback number, area and pool problem are sent to it so it can phone you back. Keep your phone close, because the return call may come from a number you do not know.

From then on you deal with the company directly. It inspects the pool, quotes its own price, does the work if you agree, and bills you. Before any repair, ask for its Arizona ROC license number and search it on the ROC contractor search (new tab). The line does not check licenses for you. The how it works page lists more questions to ask.

Call 911 first in an emergency

Gas smell near a pool heater: leave the area right away. Do not use switches, flames or anything that could make a spark. From a safe distance, call 911 and Southwest Gas at 877-860-6020.

A child or anyone in trouble in the water: call 911 now. Then keep children away from the pool until a fence, gate or cover is secure.

Never mix pool chemicals with each other, keep them in their original containers, and store them where children and pets cannot reach them. If you are unsure about a chemical or electrical problem, leave it alone and describe it to the pool company.

Your details and privacy

What you tell the assistant goes to the pool company your call reaches, so it can help you or call you back. Calls may be recorded. The privacy policy explains what is kept, who receives it and why.

Areas the line covers

The line takes calls from the City of Yuma and nearby Yuma County communities. The service areas page has notes on each one.
