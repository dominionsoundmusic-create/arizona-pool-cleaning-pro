8. Safety comes first

Pool chemicals must never be blended together. Store each product in the container it came in, in a cool, dry, shaded spot that children and pets cannot get to, and follow its label. Leave wiring, lights, pump motors and gas piping to trained professionals. A gas odor around a heater means get everyone well away first, then dial 911 and the gas company once you are at a safe distance. An unfenced or uncovered pool is no place for young children, and keep an adult's eyes on any child who is close to the water.
