Safety

Never combine pool chemicals. Keep each one in its original container, somewhere dry, shaded and locked away from children, and use it only as the label says. Pool wiring, lights, motors and gas lines are work for qualified professionals. If you smell gas near a pool heater, leave the area first and then call 911 and the gas utility from a safe distance. Keep children away from any pool without a fence or cover, and never leave a child alone near water.
