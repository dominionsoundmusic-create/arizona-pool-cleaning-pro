Everything else depends on that flow: the chlorine gets mixed in, the filter catches dust, and an automatic cleaner can move. Checks that are safe to make

  • Turn the pump off before you open anything, then empty both baskets, the one in the skimmer and the one at the front of the pump.
  • Look at the water level. When the water line drops to the lower half of the skimmer opening, air can get drawn in.
  • Look at the pump lid O-ring. A flattened, cracked or dry ring lets air in.
  • Confirm the timer or automation schedule has not shifted after a power outage.
  • Reset a tripped breaker or GFCI one time only.
  • Clear leaves, gravel and dust away from the motor so it can breathe.

Anything beyond that, such as opening the motor, touching the capacitor or changing wiring, should stay with a professional. Never run a pump dry for long to "test" it.

Repair the pump or replace it

A pool pump has a handful of parts that wear: the lid and its O-ring, the strainer basket, the impeller that moves the water, the shaft seal that keeps water away from the motor, the motor bearings, and on many single speed motors a start capacitor. Most of these can be replaced on their own. The question is whether the rest of the pump is worth keeping.

A repair often makes sense when

  • The pump is well inside its expected life, which Angi estimates at roughly 8 to 15 years.
  • The trouble is one worn part, such as a seal, an O-ring or a set of bearings.
  • The housing is sound, with no cracks.
  • The repair costs well under half the price of a new pump.

A replacement is worth pricing when

  • The repair would pass about half the cost of a new pump, the point Angi uses as a rule of thumb.
  • The motor or the housing is badly damaged.
  • The same pump keeps failing.
  • It is an older single speed pump and you want lower power bills.

The federal rule on new pump motors

Energy standards from the U.S. Department of Energy changed which motors factories can make. For motors rated 1.15 to 5 total horsepower and built from September 29, 2025 onward, variable speed control is required. Smaller motors have a later date, and in April 2026 the DOE delayed enforcement for some small motors made before March 26, 2029. Nothing in the rule forces you to remove equipment that works. It matters when a motor fails, because the replacement may have to be a variable speed model.

Running a pool pump in Yuma heat and on APS time-of-use hours

How long a pump should run is one of the most common questions, and there is no single correct answer. The idea behind it is turnover: moving the full volume of pool water through the filter. How many hours that takes depends on the size of the pool and how fast the pump moves water. A variable speed pump set to a low speed runs longer to move the same water, yet the U.S. Department of Energy's Building America guide shows it can use less energy overall. ENERGY STAR notes that a pump at half speed uses about one eighth of the energy, and suggests several short run periods if debris is a problem. The Arizona Department of Water Resources also recommends limiting pump run time to save energy. A pool technician can set a schedule that keeps your water clear without wasting power.

Who is allowed to work on a pool pump in Arizona

The state license for home pool service and minor repair work is the R-6, whose full name is Residential Swimming Pool Service and Repair. The Arizona Registrar of Contractors issues it. R-6 excludes electrical work beyond the first disconnect and gas lines, so a new circuit or rewiring belongs with a licensed electrical contractor. A careful pool technician will tell you when part of the job needs someone else.

  1. Describe the symptom. Say what you hear or see and when it started. The table above gives you the words.
  2. Let the company inspect. A technician checks flow, the seal, the motor and the electrical supply before naming a cause.
  3. Get the options in writing. Ask for the repair price and, if relevant, a replacement price.
  4. Check the license. Request the company's ROC license number and search it yourself.
