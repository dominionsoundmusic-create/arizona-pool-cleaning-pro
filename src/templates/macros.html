{%- endmacro %} {% macro license_check() -%}

Check the contractor license. Arizona's Registrar of Contractors (ROC) issues the licenses for pool work. Residential R-6, Swimming Pool Service and Repair, is the classification for servicing a home pool and handling minor repairs. Building a new pool or putting in a whole new plaster or pebble surface falls under construction classifications such as B-5. Routine cleaning on its own is a gray area the ROC has not written a clear rule on, so simply ask the company what it holds. Then type its ROC number into the ROC contractor search (new tab) to see if the license is current, what type it is and any recent complaints. Yuma Pool Cleaning Pro does not run this lookup for you.
