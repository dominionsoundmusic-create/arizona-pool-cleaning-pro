{%- endmacro %} {% macro license_check() -%}

Check the contractor license. In Arizona, contractors are licensed by the Registrar of Contractors (ROC). The residential R-6 classification, Swimming Pool Service and Repair, covers servicing home pools and minor repairs; building a pool or replacing a full plaster or pebble finish needs a construction license such as B-5. The ROC does not say plainly whether cleaning alone needs a license, so ask any company which license it holds. Take the ROC number it gives you and search it in the ROC contractor search (new tab), which shows whether the license is active, its classification and recent complaints. Yuma Pool Cleaning Pro does not check licenses for you.
